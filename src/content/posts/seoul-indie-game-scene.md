# The Growing Indie Game Scene in Seoul

Seoul has a thriving community of independent game developers creating unique experiences across a wide range of genres.

From experimental pixel-art adventures to narrative-driven games, local creators are exploring new ideas and sharing their work with players around the world.

## A Community of Creators

Indie game development is about more than making games. It's also about connecting with other creators, sharing knowledge, and learning from one another.

Community meetups give developers opportunities to:

- Showcase their latest projects.
- Receive feedback from other developers.
- Find collaborators for future games.
- Learn about tools, workflows, and publishing.

## Supporting Local Developers

At Seoul Indies, we want to make it easier to discover independent games and connect with the people behind them.

Whether you're an experienced developer or just starting your first project, there's always something new to explore.

Keep an eye on our upcoming meetups and community updates to discover what Seoul's indie game scene has to offer.
