## About

Han River Racer is a fast-paced arcade racer inspired by the streets and bridges of Seoul. Master drifting mechanics while competing in vibrant neon-lit environments.

## Features

- Arcade-style drifting
- Single-player championships
- Time Attack mode
- Original electronic soundtrack
